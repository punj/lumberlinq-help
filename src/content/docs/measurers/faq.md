**How does a tally from a measurer reach me?**

The measurer sends it from **Send to a client** using your Company ID or e-mail. You get a push notification and one e-mail per measurer (not one per tally), then find it under **Received tallies**.

**Which name do I see, the container or the measurer's Transport ID?**

Both. The container number is the main title (it is what matches your own stock), and a small blue label next to it shows the measurer's own Transport ID, for example DEMU70000900 with TM-BULK-090.

**How do I know when a tally was sent?**

Every row shows who sent it and when, in the time zone of your profile with a GMT label, and "x hours ago" for the last 24 hours. Newest tallies are on top inside each group (Need you, Ready, Waiting, Applied).

**What do the measurer's Sent tallies show?**

Waiting rows first (with **Take back**), then finished ones (Accepted, Rejected, Taken back) with the time of the answer. Use the chips, the search box and **Show more** to find older ones.

**Which count do the reports and the verify page show?**

When a Stock Unit has a Reconciliation (destination) count, the Combined report and the public verify page show that measured count first and say so. Your own count stays as the comparison.

**How do I switch the notification off?**

Account → Notifications → **Tallies from measurers**. It controls both the push and the e-mail.
