## Is the Measurer account really free?

Yes. It is free for measurement providers. It contains only tally features (Stock Unit tallies, PDF, Excel, share links, sending to clients, your staff).

## Can I use consignments, inventory or prices in it?

No. Those are not part of the Measurer account. Ask your LumberLinq contact if you want a full plan.

## Why can't my staff member work yet?

A new staff member needs **two approvals**: first the owner of your account, then LumberLinq. The owner gets a notification when someone is waiting. Until both have approved, the person sees a waiting screen.

## What can my staff see?

Only the tallies they made themselves. The owner sees all tallies of the company.

## The client says they did not get my tally.

Check on **Send to client** that you typed the right **Company ID** or email and that the company name shown is the right one. In **Sent** you can see that it was sent and, if the client has not used it yet, take it back and send it again.

## Where does a sent tally appear for the client?

As a **received** Stock Unit, marked with your company name. The client can open and use it like any other Stock Unit.

## I deleted a tally by mistake.

The owner can open the **Recycle bin** and press **Restore** within 30 days. It comes back as a new Stock Unit with the same numbers. Staff cannot restore; ask the owner.

## It says I reached the daily limit.

There is a daily limit on share links and Excel exports. Try again tomorrow. LumberLinq can raise the limit for your company; contact your LumberLinq contact.

## What is the QR code on my PDF and Excel file?

It lets anyone check that the tally is real. Scanning it opens a public page that shows the tally was measured by your company. Nobody needs to sign in.

## Can I remove the "Measured with LumberLinq" line?

No. It is part of the free account.

## How do I get more staff seats?

Share your **Invite another measurer** link from the home screen. Each measurer who joins through it gives you 5 more seats, up to 20 extra seats in total.

## I closed the Terms window without accepting.

It opens again on the next screen. The account stays closed until the Terms are accepted.
