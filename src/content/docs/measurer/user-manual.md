## What the Measurer account is

The Measurer account is a **free account for people who measure timber for others** (tally companies, surveyors, yard measurers). It is built only for tallies:

- make **Stock Unit tallies** on a phone or computer, round or square;
- share them as a **branded PDF, Excel file or share link** with a line "Measured with LumberLinq" and a QR code that anyone can scan to check the tally is real;
- **send a tally straight to a LumberLinq client** by the client's Company ID or email;
- add **your own staff**, who each see only their own tallies.

Everything else in LumberLinq (consignments, inventory, prices and so on) is switched off in this account. If you also want those, ask your LumberLinq contact about a full plan.

## How to sign up

1. Open **lumberlinq.com** and go to the measurer page (**/public/measurers**), or use the link another measurer gave you.
2. Fill in your name, company, city, country code (two letters, for example IN), email and mobile number. Press **Send**.
3. LumberLinq checks the request. When it is approved you receive an **email with your sign-in details**.
4. If the page says **"We are full for now"**, you can still leave your details. You go on the **waiting list** and are invited when a place opens.

## First sign-in

The first time you sign in a **Terms** window opens. Read it, tick the box and press **Accept**. Until you accept, the rest of the account stays closed.

## The home screen

Big buttons, one job each:

- **New tally**: starts a new Stock Unit tally. Enter the container or Transport ID and the species, then the tally grid opens.
- **My tallies**: the list of your tallies.
- **My team** (owner only): your staff.
- **Send to client**: sends a tally to a LumberLinq company.
- **Recycle bin** (owner only): tallies that were deleted.
- **Help**: this site.

Under the buttons is a short checklist: **Company ready**, **Add your team**, **Make your first tally**.

## Adding your team

1. Open **My team** and invite a person by email.
2. The person gets an invitation and creates their sign-in.
3. **Two approvals are needed before a new staff member can work:** first you (the owner) approve, then LumberLinq approves. You get a notification when someone is waiting for you, and the person gets one when they are approved.
4. Staff see **only the tallies they made themselves**. The owner sees all.

Your free account has a limited number of staff seats. Every measurer you invite who joins gives you more seats (see **Invite another measurer** below).

## Sending a tally to a client

1. Open **Send to client** and choose the tally.
2. Type the client's **Company ID** or email and press **Check**. The client's company name appears when it is a real LumberLinq company.
3. Press **Send**.
4. The client is notified and the tally appears in their account as a **received** Stock Unit, with your company name on it.
5. In **Sent** you see everything you sent and can **take back** a tally the client has not used yet.

Only companies that exist in LumberLinq can receive a tally. You never see anything of the client's own data.

## Recycle bin

When the owner deletes a tally it is kept for **30 days** in the **Recycle bin**. **Restore** brings it back as a new Stock Unit with the same numbers. After 30 days it is gone for good.

## Daily limits

To keep the free account fair, there is a daily limit on **share links** and **Excel exports** per company. When you reach it, the screen says so and you can try again the next day. LumberLinq can change the limits.

## Find a measurer and verify a tally

- **Find a measurer** (**/public/measurers/find**): a public list where companies look for measurers by name, city or country. Nobody needs to sign in.
- **Verify a tally**: scan the QR code on a PDF or Excel file. The page confirms that the tally is real and shows who measured it.

## Invite another measurer

On the home screen the owner sees an **Invite another measurer** card with a personal link. Copy it and send it. When a measurer joins through your link, you receive **5 more staff seats**, up to **20 extra seats** in total. One new company can earn you the bonus only once.

## What if something is blocked

- **"Waiting for approval"**: your sign-up or a staff member is still being checked.
- **"Accept the terms"**: open any screen and the Terms window appears; accept it.
- **"Not allowed in this account"**: that part of LumberLinq is not part of the free Measurer account.

## Tallies sent to you by a measurer

When a measurer sends you a tally, it waits in **Stock Units → Received tallies**. A bell notice tells you it arrived.

The screen lists every waiting tally. The ones that need your decision come first, then the ones that are ready, then the ones you parked. Tap a tally to see what LumberLinq found and what you can do with it.

### The three choices

- **Use for reconciliation.** Use this when you already have the container in your stock, for example from the purchase you entered at the origin. The measurer's counts become the destination side of that Stock Unit's reconciliation. This needs a plan that includes Reconciliation.
- **Add as a new Stock Unit.** Use this only for wood you have not recorded yet. The new Stock Unit carries the measurer's name and a "received" mark.
- **Keep waiting.** Nothing happens. Decide later.

### How LumberLinq finds the right container

LumberLinq compares the container number (spaces and capitals do not matter), the Transport ID, the seal and the species. When the measurer mistyped one or two characters, it suggests the closest container and shows the wrong character in red. A real container number ends in a check digit, so a mistyped number is easy to spot. You must tick that the number was mistyped before it can be used.

### Things that need your decision

- **The Stock Unit already has destination numbers.** Choose **Replace** (the old numbers stay in the history and Undo brings them back) or **Keep mine** (the measurer's tally is stored as a reference copy). There is no default.
- **The difference is more than 10%.** LumberLinq warns you and asks you to tick that you checked it is the right Stock Unit. It never blocks you.
- **Two tallies point at the same Stock Unit.** A Stock Unit takes one measurer tally at a time. Send one to another Stock Unit or keep it waiting.
- **The Stock Unit is locked.** A locked reconciliation is never changed. Pick another Stock Unit or keep the tally waiting.
- **The same container was sent twice.** The later one is marked "Sent twice" and waits until you choose which to use.

### Applying and undoing

Press **Apply** to apply everything that is ready in one go. A window lists what will happen first, and tallies that still need you are not touched. Every tally you applied appears under **Applied**. Open it and press **Undo** to take the counts off the Stock Unit and send the tally back to the list. Undo works only until you lock the reconciliation.

## Photos and files travel with the tally

When you send a tally, its photos and files go with it. The client sees them on the Stock Unit's **Photos** tab, marked "From" your company name. They are stored once, not twice. If you later delete a photo, the client keeps their copy. If the client presses Undo on a reconciliation, the shared photos leave that Stock Unit again.

## Counted by

In **Reports → Reconciliation**, a new **Counted by** column shows who made the destination count: a measurer company's name and the day, or **Your team** when your own staff counted. The Excel files say the same on the Method line.

## Origin vs Destination Report

Open **Download** on a Stock Unit or a consignment and press **Origin vs Destination Report**. It shows the origin tally against the destination count: the totals, one row per Stock Unit, a size table where the counts differ, and a summary per supplier. Units that are not counted yet are listed, but they are not part of the difference.

- **Download PDF** or **Download Excel**. No prices appear anywhere.
- **Create link for supplier** makes a link that anyone can open without signing in. It shows quantities only and stops working after 30 days. On a consignment you can pick one supplier first, so each supplier gets only their own containers.

## Compare with a client's figures (measurers)

If your client does not use LumberLinq and sent their own numbers, open **Compare with client figures** on the measurer home screen. Pick your Stock Unit, type the client's logs and CBM next to it, and add more containers if you need. **Preview** shows the difference; **Download PDF** or **Download Excel** makes the one-page report to send back. Nothing is imported or saved from the client's numbers.

## No imports in the free account

The free Measurer account has no AI Import and no Excel or CSV import. Counts are entered by hand, and a client's file is only ever compared as described above.

## Chart, links and the Destination view

- **Chart.** The Origin vs Destination Report starts with a bar chart: for each counted Stock Unit, a grey bar for the origin and a green or blue bar for the destination, with the difference beside it. For a big consignment it shows the 15 biggest differences. The PDF has the same chart.
- **Links you made.** In the Origin vs Destination Report window, **Links made for this report** lists every supplier link with its date and whether it is active. Press **Switch off** and the address stops working at once. A link also stops by itself after 30 days.
- **Origin tally / Destination tally.** On the **Tallysheet** tab of a Stock Unit that has a destination count, a switch lets you look at the destination rows. They are read-only, say who counted, and each row shows whether that size has the same number of logs at the origin. Nothing you type in the origin tally is lost when you look at the destination.
- **Photos left behind.** If a measurer deletes a photo after sending, and the client rejects the tally or the measurer takes it back, the stored photo is removed too.

## Summary and Photos tabs with a destination count

- **Summary tab:** a switch **Origin | Destination | Compare** appears. Destination shows the same summary built from the destination count. Compare shows four numbers (origin to destination, with the difference) and the volume by size band with a grey bar for the origin and a coloured bar for the destination.
- **Photos tab:** when a measurer photos came with the count, the photos are grouped under **Origin photos** and **From the destination count**.
- Stock Units without a destination count look exactly as before. The origin views are only hidden while another view is shown, so unsaved typing in the origin tally is kept.
