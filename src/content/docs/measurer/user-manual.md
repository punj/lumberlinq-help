## What the Measurer account is

The Measurer account is a **free account for people who measure timber for others** (tally companies, surveyors, yard measurers). It is built only for tallies:

- make **Stock Unit tallies** on a phone or computer, round or square;
- share them as a **branded PDF, Excel file or share link** with a line "Measured with LumberLinq" and a QR code that anyone can scan to check the tally is real;
- **send a tally straight to a LumberLinq client** by the client's Company ID or email;
- add **your own staff**, who each see only their own tallies.

Everything else in LumberLinq (consignments, inventory, prices and so on) is switched off in this account. If you also want those, ask your LumberLinq contact about a full plan.

## How to sign up

1. Open **lumberlinq.com** and go to the measurer page (**/public/measurers**), or use the link another measurer gave you.
2. Fill in your name, company, city, country code (two letters, for example IN), email and mobile number. Press **Send**.
3. LumberLinq checks the request. When it is approved you receive an **email with your sign-in details**.
4. If the page says **"We are full for now"**, you can still leave your details. You go on the **waiting list** and are invited when a place opens.

## First sign-in

The first time you sign in a **Terms** window opens. Read it, tick the box and press **Accept**. Until you accept, the rest of the account stays closed.

## The home screen

Big buttons, one job each:

- **New tally**: starts a new Stock Unit tally. Enter the container or Transport ID and the species, then the tally grid opens.
- **My tallies**: the list of your tallies.
- **My team** (owner only): your staff.
- **Send to client**: sends a tally to a LumberLinq company.
- **Recycle bin** (owner only): tallies that were deleted.
- **Help**: this site.

Under the buttons is a short checklist: **Company ready**, **Add your team**, **Make your first tally**.

## Adding your team

1. Open **My team** and invite a person by email.
2. The person gets an invitation and creates their sign-in.
3. **Two approvals are needed before a new staff member can work:** first you (the owner) approve, then LumberLinq approves. You get a notification when someone is waiting for you, and the person gets one when they are approved.
4. Staff see **only the tallies they made themselves**. The owner sees all.

Your free account has a limited number of staff seats. Every measurer you invite who joins gives you more seats (see **Invite another measurer** below).

## Sending a tally to a client

1. Open **Send to client** and choose the tally.
2. Type the client's **Company ID** or email and press **Check**. The client's company name appears when it is a real LumberLinq company.
3. Press **Send**.
4. The client is notified and the tally appears in their account as a **received** Stock Unit, with your company name on it.
5. In **Sent** you see everything you sent and can **take back** a tally the client has not used yet.

Only companies that exist in LumberLinq can receive a tally. You never see anything of the client's own data.

## Recycle bin

When the owner deletes a tally it is kept for **30 days** in the **Recycle bin**. **Restore** brings it back as a new Stock Unit with the same numbers. After 30 days it is gone for good.

## Daily limits

To keep the free account fair, there is a daily limit on **share links** and **Excel exports** per company. When you reach it, the screen says so and you can try again the next day. LumberLinq can change the limits.

## Find a measurer and verify a tally

- **Find a measurer** (**/public/measurers/find**): a public list where companies look for measurers by name, city or country. Nobody needs to sign in.
- **Verify a tally**: scan the QR code on a PDF or Excel file. The page confirms that the tally is real and shows who measured it.

## Invite another measurer

On the home screen the owner sees an **Invite another measurer** card with a personal link. Copy it and send it. When a measurer joins through your link, you receive **5 more staff seats**, up to **20 extra seats** in total. One new company can earn you the bonus only once.

## What if something is blocked

- **"Waiting for approval"**: your sign-up or a staff member is still being checked.
- **"Accept the terms"**: open any screen and the Terms window appears; accept it.
- **"Not allowed in this account"**: that part of LumberLinq is not part of the free Measurer account.
