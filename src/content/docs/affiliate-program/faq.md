---
title: Affiliate Program — FAQ
description: Common questions about applying, getting verified, and getting paid as a LumberLinq Affiliate Partner.
---

**How much commission do I earn?**

It depends on the commission plan an admin assigns you — it could be a flat amount or a percentage, and either a one-time payment or a recurring one on renewals. Your dashboard's Commission Ledger always shows your real, actual earnings, not an estimate.

**Can I start sharing my referral link before I'm approved?**

Yes. Your referral link is active as soon as you apply, even while your application shows as Pending Review — you don't need to wait for approval to start sharing it.

**Why can't a payout be recorded for me yet?**

Two things are required first: your verification (Legal & Contact Details, Tax ID, and Documents) must be complete, and you need a payout account saved. Check the Affiliate → Verification page for your current status and any rejection reason if something needs fixing.

**What's the difference between "Held" and "Eligible" commission?**

"Held" commission is still in its hold period after a referral's payment — it hasn't cleared yet. "Eligible" means it's cleared and ready to be paid out. Only Eligible commission can actually be paid.

**My commission is stuck — what happened?**

If your payout currency doesn't match the currency your referral paid in, a percentage-based commission can be held waiting for an admin to apply an exchange rate before it can be finalized. This is normal for cross-currency referrals and doesn't need any action from you.

**Can I sign up as an affiliate if I already have a LumberLinq subscription?**

Yes — apply from the "Become an Affiliate" page while logged in (`/affiliate/apply`), which skips the email/password step since you're already logged in.

**A document I uploaded for verification was rejected — what now?**

Check the reason shown next to that document on the Verification page, then use the **Replace** button to upload a corrected version. There's no limit on re-uploads.

**Is my payout information secure?**

Yes — your saved payout account details and tax ID are stored masked and only shown in full when you explicitly click Reveal.
