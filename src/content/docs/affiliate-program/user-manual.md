## What Is the Affiliate Program?

The Affiliate Program lets you earn commission by referring new subscribers to LumberLinq. You get a personal referral link — anyone who signs up through it is tracked as your referral, and you earn commission (a flat amount or a percentage, depending on the commission plan an admin assigns you) on their payments.

## How to Apply

There are two ways to apply, depending on whether you already use LumberLinq:

**If you don't have a LumberLinq account yet:**

1. Go to the public **Become an Affiliate** page (`/join-as-affiliate`).
2. Under **Your Account**, enter your **Email** and a **Password** (at least 8 characters) — this creates your login.
3. Under **About You**, fill in your **Display name** (required), and optionally your **Business name** and **Contact phone**.
4. Under **Payout Details**, enter your **Country code** and choose your **Payout currency**. You'll add your actual bank/UPI/PayPal details later, once you're approved.
5. Optionally add a note in **Anything else?**.
6. Tick the agreement checkbox confirming you accept the LumberLinq Affiliate Partner Agreement and that your details are accurate.
7. Click **Submit Application** — or, instead of filling the form manually, use one of the **social sign-up** buttons to continue with Google/another provider (this still requires the country, currency, and agreement fields above to be filled first).

**If you're already a LumberLinq subscriber:**

1. While logged in, go to the **Become an Affiliate** page (`/affiliate/apply`).
2. The form is the same as above, minus the Email/Password section — you're already logged in.
3. Fill in your details and submit.

After submitting, you'll see an **Application submitted** confirmation: *"Thanks for applying to the LumberLinq Affiliate Program. A SUPER_ADMIN will review your application soon — you'll be notified as soon as a decision is made."* If you applied without an existing account, you'll get a **Go to Login** button.

## Your Application Status

Once approved, you can sign in and reach your **Affiliate Dashboard**. While your application is still **Pending Review**, the dashboard shows a banner letting you know — and importantly, **your referral link already works during this time**, so you can start sharing it before approval comes through.

## Your Affiliate Dashboard

Your dashboard (reached after logging in as an approved affiliate) shows:

- **Your Referral Link** — your personal link, with buttons to **Copy** it, **share via WhatsApp**, or show a **QR code** to scan.
- Four stat tiles: **Total Referred** (how many companies signed up through your link), **Held** (commission still in its hold period), **Eligible** (commission cleared and ready for payout), and **Lifetime Paid** (total paid out to you so far).
- Four tabs:
  - **Overview** — a summary, or a "Share your link to get started" prompt if you have no referrals yet.
  - **Referrals** — every company that signed up through your link, with signup date, how they were attributed, and their status.
  - **Commission Ledger** — every commission entry, with date, amount, status, and the date it becomes eligible for payout.
  - **Payout History** — every payout actually made to you, with date, amount, and reference number.

If verification isn't complete yet, a banner reminds you: *"Complete your verification before your first payout can be recorded — takes a couple of minutes,"* with a button straight to the verification page.

## Completing Verification

Go to **Affiliate → Verification** (or click the banner on your dashboard). This has three sections, each saved independently:

1. **Legal & Contact Details** — your legal full name, address (line 1, line 2 optional, city, state, postal code, country), and optional alternate email/phone. This doesn't have to match your public display name — it's just to confirm a payout is genuinely yours. Click **Save Details**.
2. **Tax Identification** — add your **PAN** (for India) or a **Foreign tax ID** if you're outside India. Once saved, it's shown masked, with an eye icon to reveal it again if you need to check it. Click **Save Tax ID**.
3. **Documents** — upload up to 3 supporting documents (PDF, JPG, or PNG, max 10 MB each). Each shows its verification status once reviewed. If a document is rejected, the reason is shown so you can re-upload a corrected one.

Your overall verification status shows as **Not started**, **Under review**, **Verified**, or **Needs changes** (with the specific reason shown if something needs fixing).

## Adding Your Payout Account

Go to **Affiliate → Payout Account**. Choose a payout **Method**:

- **India Bank** — bank name, account holder name, account number, IFSC code.
- **India UPI** — your UPI ID (e.g. `name@bank`).
- **Foreign Bank Wire** — bank name, beneficiary name, account number/IBAN, SWIFT/BIC, and the bank's address.
- **PayPal** — your PayPal email.
- **Wise** — your Wise account identifier.

Fill in the fields for your chosen method and click **Save**. Once saved, your payout details are shown masked on this page, with a **Reveal**/**Hide** toggle so you can double-check them without them being visible by default.

## When Do I Actually Get Paid?

Commission on a referral's payment doesn't get released immediately — it goes through a hold period first (shown as **Held** in your ledger), then becomes **Eligible**, and is finally recorded as **Paid** once an admin processes the payout. You need both your verification completed and a payout account on file before a payout can be recorded against you.
