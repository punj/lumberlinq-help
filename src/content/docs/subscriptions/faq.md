**Payment was debited but my plan didn't upgrade — what do I do?**

Wait 2-3 minutes for the webhook to confirm with our server. If the plan is still not updated after 10 minutes, raise a Support Ticket with your transaction ID — our team can verify and manually activate the plan.

**I can't access a feature right after upgrading — is that expected?**

Try logging out and back in to refresh your session first. If the issue persists, raise a Support Ticket.

**My AI Chat (Linc) messages ran out today — when do they reset?**

Daily AI Chat limits reset at midnight (server time). To get a higher daily limit, upgrade to a higher plan.

**My promo code isn't working — why?**

Make sure it's entered exactly as given (promo codes are case-sensitive) and check whether it has an expiry date or is limited to specific plans.

**What happens when my trial ends?**

Nothing is deleted — all your existing tally sheets, consignments, and other records stay exactly as they are. Your account downgrades to a lower, limited plan: you can keep using and editing everything you already have, you just can't create new records past that lower plan's limits until you subscribe again. Unlike a lapsed paid plan (which gets a short grace period first), a trial ends and downgrades right on its end date, with no extra window.

**What payment methods are supported?**

The subscription checkout screen uses Razorpay or Cashfree (UPI, net banking, cards, depending on your region) — whichever is configured for your account.

**How do I download an invoice?**

Subscription → Payment History → click the download icon next to any paid invoice.

**What happens if I cancel my subscription?**

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, not immediately. Your data is retained until the account is fully deactivated.
