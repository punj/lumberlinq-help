LumberLinq has two kinds of video calls. They look alike, but they are for different things.

| | **Screen support** | **Video call with the LumberLinq team** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Support looks at your LumberLinq screen to solve a problem | A call with a LumberLinq sales person, for example a demo |
| How it starts | Inside a **support ticket** | A **link** sent to you by e-mail or message |
| Do you need to be signed in? | Yes | No |
| Recorded? | **Never** | **Never** |

---

## Screen support (you are signed in)

Support can ask to see your LumberLinq screen while they help you with a ticket. **You are always in control.** Nothing starts until you press the buttons yourself.

### When support asks to see your screen

1. You get a **notification** (and a ticket reply) saying "Support would like to see your screen".
2. A window opens on your screen. Press **Open now** (or open the ticket and press **Share my screen**).
3. The call opens full screen. Press the **share screen** button in the call.
4. In your browser's picker choose **the LumberLinq tab only**. Do not choose "Entire screen" or another window.
5. Support sees only what you chose. They **cannot click or type** for you.
6. To finish, press **Stop** at the top. Support can also end the session.

If you are not ready, press **Not now**. The invitation stays in the ticket for 30 minutes.

### If you want to ask for it

If your company has this switched on, a **company administrator** will see **Ask support to see my LumberLinq tab** inside a ticket and on the ticket list. Support then accepts it and the call opens in the same way. If you do not see the button, raise a normal ticket and support will start the session for you.

### Rules to know

- Only **the person support asked** can join, not other people in your company.
- You have **30 minutes** to join after support asks. A session lasts at most **60 minutes**.
- Only **one** screen support session can be open for your company at a time.
- It is **never recorded**, and nothing stays open after you press Stop.
- **A phone or the LumberLinq app cannot share a screen.** This is a limit of phones, not of LumberLinq. You can still join, talk and chat. To share your screen, use **Chrome or Edge on a computer**.

## Video call with the LumberLinq team (you got a link)

1. Open the link from your e-mail or message. Use a computer or a phone with a camera and microphone.
2. If the invitation came by e-mail, press **Send me a code**. We send a **6-digit code to the e-mail address the invitation went to**. Type the code. (A link you were sent by message may not ask for a code.)
3. Type your **name** and **e-mail**, tick the security check and press **Join**.
4. **Wait to be let in.** The host admits each person. This can take a moment.
5. Allow the **camera and microphone** when your browser asks.

### Rules to know

- **The link is for you only. Please do not forward it.** If someone else needs to join, ask the LumberLinq team to invite them.
- The link works for a short time (about 2 hours) and only a few times. If it has expired, ask for a new one.
- The call is **never recorded**.
- You cannot send files in the call unless the host allows it.

## Check your setup before you join

On the video call page press **Check my setup**. It tests, in a few seconds, your browser, your camera, your microphone and whether you can share a screen, and tells you what to fix.

## Fixing common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page says the link has expired or was used too many times | Ask the LumberLinq team for a new link |
| "Waiting for the host" | The host has to press Admit. Wait a moment, or message the host |
| The code e-mail does not arrive | Check the spam folder. The code goes to the address the invitation was sent to, not to a different address you type. Press **Send again** after 30 seconds |
| "That code is not right" | Type the newest code. A code works for 10 minutes and for 5 tries. Press **Send again** for a new one |
| Camera or microphone does not work | Click the camera or lock icon in the browser's address bar, choose **Allow**, then refresh the page |
| The call is stuck on "Starting the call…" | Check your internet, then press **Retry**. If it still waits, press **Open in a new tab** |
| I can't share my screen | You are on a phone or in the app. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer |
| I shared the wrong window | Press **Stop** and start again. Choose only the LumberLinq tab |
| I do not see "Screen support" in my ticket | Your company may not have it switched on. Ask support in the ticket to start the session |

Still stuck? Raise a ticket from **Support** in the menu and tell us what you see on the screen.
