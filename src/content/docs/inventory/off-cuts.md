*Also searched as: off-cuts, offcuts, leftover pieces, wastage pieces, small pieces, short lengths, quiet day trimming, rough batch, approx stock, kaat, tukda.*

**Off-cuts** is for the small pieces that come out of sawing. Most mills turn them into short lengths on a quiet day and sell them by the container. You usually know the total (about 3 CBM of 1 to 5.75 ft pieces) but not each piece. Off-cuts lets you record that rough total as stock, and the exact measuring happens later, when the pieces are tallied into a container.

> Off-cuts is a **paid optional add-on**. If you do not see the **Off-cuts** button on Inventory → Overview, ask your LumberLinq contact to switch it on for your company. Inventory must be on too.

## Add off-cuts

1. Open **Inventory → Overview** and press **Off-cuts**.
2. **Step 1: What are they?**
   - **Roughly**: a total volume for a length range. It is measured exactly when you load a container.
   - **Piece by piece**: exact lengths and pieces, like a tally sheet.
   - Pick the **product** (sawn products only) and **where they are stored**. If they came from a finished mill job, pick it under **From mill job** (optional).
3. **Step 2: Assign to operator** (optional): pick the person who will record the sizes after sawing, a machine and a note. The person gets a notification.
   - **Nobody assigned**: you enter the sizes yourself in step 3, and the batches are created at once.
   - **Someone assigned**: a task is created. The operator enters the sizes after sawing.
4. **Step 3: Sizes and volume**: one row per size and length range.

### The size grid

- **Units:** thickness and width are in inches and length in feet by default. The **Size** and **Length** headers have a small unit button: mm, cm, m, in or ft. When you change a unit, the numbers you already typed are converted.
- **Volume** can be typed as **CBM**, **CFT** or **pcs** (pieces need a length range or an average length).
- **Average length** is optional. Left empty, the middle of From and To is used to estimate the pieces.
- **Enter** moves to the next cell; after Volume it adds a new row. Negative numbers are not accepted.
- On a phone each size is a card with big fields, and **+ Add size** adds the next one.
- **Ctrl + S** (Mac: **Cmd + S**) saves in the dialog.

## The operator's task

The assigned person finds the task on **Inventory → Overview** (or **My Tasks**) and presses **Open**.

- **Grid** or **Operator** view. Operator view has big **−** and **+** tiles. Long tap (or the pencil) on a size, a length or an amount to type it.
- **Save** (Ctrl + S, or Cmd + S on a Mac) keeps what you typed and stays on the page. You can leave and come back, also from another device.
- **Done: create off-cuts** creates the batches and closes the task.
- A scroll that starts on **−** or **+** never changes the amount.

The office can press the pencil on an open task (on Overview) to change its place, operator, machine or note, or to cancel the task. The product and the way of measuring cannot change: cancel the task and make a new one.

## What you get

One **batch** (lot) per size and length range, on **Stock Lots** with an **Off-cuts** chip. A rough batch also shows **Approx.** until a load measures it. A finished mill job shows its off-cuts under **Off-cuts** in the job details, with the percentage of the input.

If the add-on ends later, your existing off-cuts batches stay and can still be sold. Only making new ones stops.

## When a container is loaded

Tally the load on a Stock Out as usual. When you press **Confirm Stock-Out**:

1. If a rough batch is **too small** for what you tallied, one question shows **In stock → Loaded = Add to batch**. Press **Add and continue** to add the difference. It is recorded as *Estimate corrected* in the stock history. If the difference is very large, a warning asks you to check the sizes first.
2. You then see the normal confirmation and confirm.
3. If a rough batch still holds wood afterwards, one question asks: **Keep it** (the batch stays and becomes *Measured*) or **Close batch** (the leftover is written off).

The load always takes exactly the volume you tallied.

## Common problems

- **No Off-cuts button:** the add-on is not on for your company, or you do not have the right to receive stock.
- **A pieces number looks too high or low:** a rough batch estimates pieces from the length range. Set the **Average length** for a closer estimate.
- **I picked the wrong product:** cancel the task (pencil on Overview) and add it again.
